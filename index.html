Active Jobs Dashboards

PL-JOBS-DASHBOARDS

Exploring what "my active jobs" looks like for different roles — driven by the scan-location data in job_locations.xlsx for individuals and departments, and by client attachment for sales.

Prototypes

My Active Jobs — Creative & Production

Per-person dashboard for anyone with a named scan location (e.g. Colin Dunn, Kristen M). Switch the "Viewing as" picker to preview anyone.

Department Jobs — Bindery, Woodshop & the rest of Production

One shared board per department. Every scan location in that department is a filter pill on the toolbar.

My Clients' Active Jobs — Sales

Jobs scoped to the client accounts a rep carries, not to a scan location. Pills filter by client instead.
